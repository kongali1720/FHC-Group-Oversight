# FHC Group KPI Framework

## 1. Purpose

Establish a consistent framework for measuring performance,
operational discipline, financial health, and risk across FHC
and its business units.

## 2. Oversight Principles

- Each business unit is responsible for accurate source data.
- FHC consolidates reports and monitors performance.
- Every KPI must have an owner, target, reporting frequency,
  data source, and corrective action where needed.
- Targets must be approved by authorized management.
- Actual results must be supported by verifiable records.

## 3. Group-Level KPI Categories

### Finance
- Monthly reporting timeliness
- Budget versus actual expenditure
- Cash-flow forecast variance
- Overdue receivables and payables

### Operations
- Revenue and gross margin
- Operating cost variance
- Production or sales performance
- Inventory control where applicable

### Governance and Risk
- Reporting compliance
- Open high-priority risks
- Overdue corrective actions
- Internal control exceptions

### Investment Oversight
- Investment opportunities reviewed
- Due-diligence completion
- Approved versus actual deployment
- Project milestone performance

## 4. Reporting Cycle

- Monthly: business-unit performance reporting
- Monthly: FHC consolidated management review
- Quarterly: strategic and investment review
- As needed: escalation of material risks or incidents

## 5. KPI Record Requirements

Each KPI record should include:

- KPI name and definition
- Responsible owner
- Target and measurement unit
- Actual result and reporting period
- Data source and evidence
- Status and variance explanation
- Corrective action, owner, and due date

## 6. Status Classification

- Green: target achieved or within approved tolerance
- Amber: deviation requiring management attention
- Red: material deviation, missed target, or critical risk
- Grey: data unavailable or not yet validated

Status thresholds must be defined and approved for each KPI.

## 7. Governance

This framework is a working draft. Management must approve
targets, thresholds, reporting responsibilities, and escalation
rules before operational adoption.

Do not store confidential investor, banking, KYC, or contract
records in a public repository.
