# FHC KPI Dictionary

**Status:** Draft — targets and thresholds require management approval.

Each KPI must have a named owner, formula, unit of measure, reporting frequency, source evidence, and escalation rule. The list below is a proposed starting point; tailor operational KPIs to each unit's actual business model.

## Group finance KPIs

| KPI | Definition / formula | Frequency | Proposed owner | Evidence / control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Sum of recognized revenue for the reporting period, using the approved accounting basis | Monthly | Unit Finance / Finance Division | Ledger, invoices, sales report |
| Gross Profit | Revenue − cost of sales | Monthly | Finance Division | Trial balance and cost records |
| Gross Margin % | Gross profit ÷ revenue × 100; mark N/A if revenue is zero | Monthly | Finance Division | Recalculate from verified figures |
| Operating Expense Variance | Actual operating expense − approved budget | Monthly | Unit Head / Finance Division | Approved budget and ledger |
| Budget Utilization % | Actual expenditure ÷ approved budget × 100; N/A if budget is zero | Monthly | Finance Division | Budget version and ledger |
| Closing Cash | Reconciled closing cash and bank balances for the period | Monthly | Treasury / Finance Division | Bank reconciliation and cashbook |
| Cash Forecast Variance | Actual cash movement − forecast cash movement | Monthly | Treasury | Forecast version and bank/cash records |
| Overdue Receivables | Sum of receivables past due date at reporting cut-off | Monthly | Finance / Credit Control | Aging report and invoices |
| Overdue Payables | Sum of payables past due date at reporting cut-off | Monthly | Finance / Procurement | Aging report and supplier statements |
| Reporting Timeliness | Reports received on time ÷ reports expected × 100 | Monthly | Group Oversight | Submission log |

## Performance and control KPIs

| KPI | Definition / formula | Frequency | Proposed owner | Evidence / control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KPI Achievement % | Actual ÷ target × 100 where higher is better; use a KPI-specific formula for lower-is-better metrics | Monthly | KPI owner | Approved target and validated result |
| Open High Risks | Count of open risks rated high/critical under the approved risk method | Monthly | Risk owner / Group Oversight | Risk register |
| Overdue Actions | Count of corrective actions past due and not closed | Monthly | Action owners / Group Oversight | Action tracker |
| Control Exceptions | Count of material exceptions identified during the period | Monthly | Control owner | Exception log and evidence |
| Due Diligence Completion | Completed required due-diligence items ÷ required items × 100 | Per investment / monthly | Investment team | Controlled due-diligence checklist |
| Project Milestone Delivery | Milestones completed on time ÷ milestones due × 100 | Monthly | Project owner | Approved project plan and acceptance evidence |

## Unit-specific KPI examples

- **DA-B:** production volume, quality grade, saleable output, realized selling price, operating cost per unit.
- **DA-RS:** sales volume, gross margin, stock availability, inventory aging, compliance exceptions.
- **DA-MI:** sales per store, gross margin, stock turnover, shrinkage, out-of-stock rate.
- **DA-Food:** production yield, cost per unit, waste rate, on-time delivery, product returns.
- **HGA:** production volume, recovery/yield where relevant, operating cost per unit, safety incidents, permit/compliance status.

These are examples only. Confirm the units' actual activities and legal scope before adopting them.

## Status and escalation

- Green / Amber / Red / Grey must follow approved KPI-specific thresholds.
- Grey means missing, unvalidated, or not applicable; never treat it as zero.
- A red KPI or critical risk must have an action owner, due date, mitigation, and escalation record.
- Document any target changes, including approver, effective date, and rationale.
- Do not combine currencies or reporting periods without documented conversion and consolidation rules.
