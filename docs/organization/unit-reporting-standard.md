# FHC Group Unit Reporting Standard

**Status:** Proposed functional standard; management approval required.

## 1. Purpose

Establish a consistent reporting process for all working business units
under the FHC Group Oversight framework.

This standard supports group-level visibility, financial consolidation,
performance monitoring, risk escalation, and management action tracking.
It does not replace the legal responsibilities of individual entities.

## 2. Scope

This standard applies to the following working unit identifiers:

- DA-B — Swiftlet / edible bird's nest business
- DA-RS — Cigarette business
- DA-MI — Minimarket business
- DA-Food — Snack / food business
- HGA — Mining business

These identifiers and descriptions are provisional until management
verifies the registered entities, ownership, operational scope, and
applicable licences.

## 3. Reporting Responsibilities

Each unit must designate an accountable unit owner and report preparer.
A reviewer and authorized approver must be identified by management.

Group Oversight coordinates consolidated reporting and follows up
management actions. It does not automatically acquire authority to
approve transactions, sign contracts, operate bank accounts, or make
statutory declarations on behalf of an individual legal entity.

## 4. Minimum Monthly Submission

Each unit should submit:

1. Executive summary and material developments.
2. Revenue, operating expenses, cash position, and budget variances.
3. Operational results and unit-specific KPIs.
4. Material contracts, commitments, and outstanding obligations.
5. Risks, incidents, exceptions, and required escalation.
6. Decisions and management actions, including owner and due date.
7. Supporting schedules and reconciliation status, where applicable.

Items that do not apply must be marked "N/A"; missing information must
not be represented as zero or as a confirmed result.

## 5. Submission and Review

The group reporting calendar defines the applicable submission dates.
The unit owner is responsible for completeness and timely submission.
The designated reviewer documents questions and unresolved variances.

Group Oversight consolidates submissions, tracks exceptions, and presents
material issues for management review.

Deadlines, materiality thresholds, reporting currencies, and approval
limits must be formally confirmed by management.

## 6. Confidentiality and Records

Investor information, bank records, identity documents, payroll,
contracts, and other confidential source records must be stored in
approved restricted-access storage, not in a public repository.

Repository documents should contain only approved procedures, blank
templates, and non-sensitive governance guidance.

## 7. Entity Verification

Before external or legal use, management must verify the legal name,
registration details, jurisdiction, ownership, directors, licences,
tax registrations, reporting currency, and authorized approvers of
each relevant entity.

## 8. Approval

**Status:** Proposed — pending management review and approval.

Approver: To be designated by management.
Effective date: To be confirmed.
Review frequency: At least annually, and when material changes occur.
