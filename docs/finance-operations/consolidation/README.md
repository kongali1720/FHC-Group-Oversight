# FHC Group Finance Consolidation

## Purpose
Provide a consistent management-level view of financial
performance across participating FHC business units.

## Reporting Units
- DA-B
- DA-RS
- DA-MI
- DA-Food
- HGA

## Required Monthly Data
- Revenue
- Cost of sales
- Gross profit
- Operating expenses
- Net profit or loss
- Opening and closing cash balances
- Receivables and payables
- Budget versus actual
- Material risks and exceptions

## Consolidation Controls
1. Identify the legal entity and reporting currency for each record.
2. Confirm that reporting periods are consistent.
3. Reconcile source reports to supporting accounting records.
4. Identify intercompany balances and transactions.
5. Investigate material discrepancies before consolidation.
6. Record review, approval, and any adjustment.
7. Restrict access to confidential financial records.

## Reporting Principles
- Keep each entity's books and bank accounts distinct.
- Do not treat investor commitments as received cash.
- Do not count the same intercompany transaction twice
  in consolidated group results.
- Document consolidation adjustments and their approval.
- Apply the accounting framework approved for the group.

## Approval
This document is a working procedure and requires
management approval before formal implementation.
