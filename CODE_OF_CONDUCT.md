# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to maintain a professional, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone contributing to this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Respect confidentiality and data-protection requirements.
- Clearly distinguish facts, assumptions, proposals, and approvals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or confidential information without authorization.
- Deliberately misleading contributors about approvals, financial status, or regulatory compliance.
- Sharing credentials or sensitive personal data.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed and handled proportionately, with privacy respected to the extent possible.
